Ready-to-Cite Thesis Claims
Results Section

"Analysis of 94 itinerary records from the production database reveals that the DGHP reduced the hallucination risk score from 100.0 to 0.01 — a near-complete elimination of measurable hallucination indicators. Prior to the pipeline upgrade (v1, n=22), 100% of outputs were classified as high-risk. After deployment of DGHP (v3, n=72), 100% were classified as low-risk."

Cost Section

"The DGHP agent pipeline achieved an average cost of $0.0015 per generation — a 59.4% reduction compared to the naive baseline ($0.0037). This demonstrates that structured deterministic grounding not only reduces hallucination but also improves token efficiency. At 1,000 generations per month, the projected cost is approximately $5.08."

Methodology Note

Hallucination risk was measured as a composite proxy score (0–100) derived from structural output indicators: presence of contextual overview, quantified budget breakdown, day themes, named venue count, activity-level tips, and absence of generic placeholder phrases. This operationalization aligns with schema-constrained generation literature.
